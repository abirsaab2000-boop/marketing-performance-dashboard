Marketing Performance Dashboard

Every number on this page is split by lead source, so you can compare one source against another at each step: how many leads it brings, how far its deals get, how often they convert, and how much revenue it closes. Sources marked paid are paid ad campaigns.

1Lead volume

New leads by source

Contacts added to GoHighLevel in this period, grouped by their Source field. Highest first. Hover a bar for the count.

2Pipeline

Where each source's deals are today

Each bar is one source's deals in the pipeline that were created in this period, split by the stage they sit in now. Bars show shares so sources of different sizes compare fairly. The number of deals is on the right. Hover a bar for counts.

3Conversion

Conversion funnel for each source

One funnel per source, all drawn on the same scale. The top band is all of that source's deals, and each band below is how many reached the next step. The percentage between two bands is the conversion rate for that step. Sources are in the same order as in Lead volume. Hover a band or a rate for the counts.
